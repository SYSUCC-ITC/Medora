## MEDORA Usage Instructions
MEDORA (Modular & Extensible Dynamic Orchestration for Real-time AI in medicine), a workflow-driven framework that facilitates the agile transformation of general-purpose LLMs into multi-scenario clinical agents through non-invasive, real-time data coupling with existing hospital infrastructures.
As the MEDORA framework is natively integrated with internal hospital infrastructures, the live operational environment cannot be shared externally. To facilitate peer review and ensure the reproducibility of our findings, a public-facing version of the MEDORA platform has been established (https://ai-platform.yiducloud.com.cn/). A static, fully anonymized version of the patient datasets has been imported into this platform, and the specific clinical agents utilized in this research have been re-established within this environment. Access credentials for the demonstration account can be obtained by contacting the corresponding author, Y.S., via email at sunying@sysucc.org.cn.
The usage guide will provide instructions on how to view and utilize the pre-established agents on the MEDORA, as well as how to create agents on the MEDORA.
## Video of MEDORA's use in real environment
As the MEDORA framework is natively integrated with internal hospital infrastructures, the live operational environment is inaccessible to external users due to strict network security and patient privacy protocols. To provide transparency and demonstrate the system’s real-world utility, we have provided video demonstrations (Supplementary Videos) showcasing the execution of MEDORA-based agents within the authentic clinical environment
